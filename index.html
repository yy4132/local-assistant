Yong-Jin's Local Assistant

Personal, locally hosted AI assistant

About

Yong-Jin's Local Assistant is a privately operated application designed to assist its owner with selected email monitoring, information retrieval, and personal administrative tasks.

Features

  • Read-only Gmail integration for authorized accounts
  • Monitoring of selected academic and administrative emails
  • Financial notice review with human verification
  • Local AI chat and saved information lookup
  • Notification history and data retention controls

Google Account Access

The application requests Gmail read-only authorization from its owner. It does not request permission to send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Privacy and Availability

The assistant runs on its owner's computer and is not publicly hosted. This website provides information about the application only.
