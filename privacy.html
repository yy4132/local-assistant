Privacy Policy

Yong-Jin's Local Assistant

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

Yong-Jin's Local Assistant is a personal, locally hosted application designed for email monitoring, information retrieval, administrative assistance, and user-reviewed financial reminders.

2. Google Account Permissions

The application accesses Gmail only after the account owner explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth 2.0.

The requested scope is:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

This permission permits read-only access to authorized Gmail messages and associated data. The application does not request permission to send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

3. Information Collected and Used

Depending on the configured monitoring workflow, the application may retrieve email headers, sender addresses, message content, timestamps, and attachment information.

Information is used to:

  • Monitor selected senders and messages.
  • Identify relevant academic and administrative notifications.
  • Review insurance and financial notices.
  • Retrieve saved email evidence with source references.
  • Generate user-reviewed reminders.

4. Local Storage and Retention

The application stores its working data locally on the owner's computer.

The default retention period for email content and email-derived evidence conversations is 30 days.

Local backups may be retained until manually deleted. Certain deduplication and verified reminder records may be retained according to the application's settings.

5. AI Processing

The application supports optional local model generation. Gmail message bodies are not submitted to the local language model for generation under the current design.

Model-generated responses cannot independently authorize actions or financial transactions.

6. Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. It does not sell Gmail data, use Gmail data for advertising, or intentionally publish private email content.

Data is exchanged with Google services as necessary to authenticate authorized accounts and retrieve requested Gmail information.

7. Security

OAuth credentials and locally stored records must be protected by the security controls of the owner's computer and application.

Publicly hosted informational pages do not contain Gmail credentials, access tokens, or private email content.

8. User Control and Revocation

The account owner may disable monitoring, manage saved information, and revoke Google authorization at any time.

Google authorization can be managed at: Google Account Connections
